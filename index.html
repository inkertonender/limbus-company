!! FAQ: PLEASE READ THIS FIRST !!

Why grind when I can just do my weeklies? Only do this if you want to get IDs / EGOs as fast as possible. The fastest team clears Mirror Dungeon Hard under 15 minutes. 3 Limbus Pass levels in 15-20 minutes means you can dispense any ID / EGO you want in 5-6 hours (15-18 hours without paid pass).
Is this beginner friendly? What if I lack the respective IDs / EGOs? NO. Most teams are NOT beginner friendly. Most people grind Mirror Dungeon after they have completed the Story (when they start stocking modules). The teams here require specific IDs / EGOs to work, if you lack them I recommend that you check Borderlined's MD6 Beginner's Guide (links below).
Recommended brainless team to grind with? Burn. You basically Win-Rate, fuse for Glimpse, and find Drumsticks. Takes 16-18 minutes to do a full Mirror Dungeon. Also you can pick Burn units that aren't in the Season 5 so it is flexible.

Why are my runs Starlight Negative? What are my solutions? We run in 6 Team rotations to get 85-90 or even 120 Rest Bonus, this means using 6 similar or different teams with 5-6 different bench IDs for additional Starlight. This gives more leeway to strats and flexibility to the Cost Economy. Almost every team here can be Starlight Neutral or Positive while doing this, you can also just use different Statuses per run.
