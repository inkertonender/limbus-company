Why are my runs Starlight Negative?

What are my solutions?		We run in 6 Team rotations to get 85-90 or even 120 Rest Bonus, this means using 6 similar or different teams with 5-6 different bench IDs for additional Starlight. This gives more leeway to strats and flexibility to the Cost Economy.

Almost every team here can be Starlight Neutral or Positive while doing this, you can also just use different Statuses per run.
